Participants are federation actors (not users). Approved outputs are governance metadata describing what a participant approved for external exposure. Read-only view.

These are synthetic demo records. The participants and approvals below were created by this application's demo seed to make the governance model visible. They are not real Data Collaboration Agreements and are not evidence that any real organisation approved anything.
The Exposed Outputs table is the safe output boundary: it shows only approved, non-expired fields of active participants — never whole datasets, and never scenario outputs (which are never auto-approved).

That is why the three tables do not agree, and the disagreement is the point: an approval held by an inactive participant, and a revoked approval, both appear under Approved Outputs but are deliberately absent from Exposed Outputs.
